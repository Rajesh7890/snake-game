## 🎮 How to Play
- **Desktop**: Use the **Arrow Keys** (Up, Down, Left, Right) to navigate the snake.
- **Mobile**: Use the on-screen **D-Pad** controls to steer the snake.
- Avoid hitting the walls and do not run into your own tail!

## 🏆 Scoring & Mechanics
The game gets dynamically faster as you score more points. Watch out for the special items that spawn occasionally!

- 🍎 **Normal Food (Red)**
  - Grants **+10 Points**.
  - Slightly increases game speed.

- 🪙 **Bonus (Gold Coin)**
  - Appears occasionally and stays for 10 seconds.
  - Grants **+50 Points**.
  - Significantly increases game speed!

- 💣 **Bomb (Black with Red Fuse)**
  - Appears occasionally and stays for 10 seconds.
  - **Halves** your current score!
  - Cuts your snake's length in half.
  - Significantly *decreases* game speed, giving you a chance to recover.

## 🥇 Leaderboard
The game features a session-based leaderboard. Enter your name before starting a game. Your top 5 runs (ranked by score, then survival time) will be displayed next to the game canvas!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- HTML5 Canvas
- CSS3 (Responsive Flexbox, Media Queries)
